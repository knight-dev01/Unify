Definition of Laplace Transform

The Laplace transform of a function f(t) is defined as a semi-infinite integral. The parameter s is assumed positive so the integral converges.

Laplace Transform Definition

L{f(t)} = ∫₀^∞ f(t) e^(−st) dt

The integral converges when the real part of s is large enough and positive.
