Laplace Transformation

Differential equationsControl theory
1.1

Solving ODEs the hard way

A linear ODE with constant coefficients is usually solved by finding a general solution, then applying boundary conditions to find the constants.

The Laplace transform turns the ODE into an algebraic equation, and builds the boundary conditions in from the start.

1.2

Definition

The transform of f(t) is a semi-infinite integral. The parameter s is taken as positive so it converges.

Laplace transform definition

L{f(t)} = ∫₀^∞ f(t) e^(−st) dt

Converges when the real part of s is large enough.
