The social post template at 1080 × 1080, built from the 221 Students impact post with the brand fixes applied.

The consumer provides the quote, the stat or headline, supporting lines, the checklist or steps, the CTA, the footer line, and a Box Boy cut-out image.

Fixed on every post:
- `green` bar on the right edge, 72px wide, top to about 60% of the height.
- Lockup top-left at `space-10`: `wordmark` at 36px, `tagline` in Playfair under it.
- Footer: 52px `near-black` strip with a `poster-strip` line, or the social handles row.
- Box Boy cut-out bottom-right, overlapping the bar, 40 to 45% of the width.

Content:
- One Playfair moment: `quote` in italic at 46px, second line in `green-deep`.
- Stat in `poster-stat` `green`, headline in `poster-headline`, sub-line in `poster-sub` with the key phrase in `green-deep`.
- Checklist card: `near-black`, `radius-xl`, `green` check circles with `on-green`, `invert-border` dividers, a `green` CTA bar inside.

Ground is `off-white`, never pure white. No emoji. Posts always use the light palette, whatever the viewer's theme.
